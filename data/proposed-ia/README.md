# Proposed IA (not in use)

The proposed IA the prototypes used until v1.2.1 (the reviewed IA from 15 Oct 2026, with later changes: Giving out of the core, the two institutes added next to Oceans Institute on 20 Oct). From v1.3.0 (22 Oct 2026) every prototype uses the existing site's IA instead (`data/sitemap.csv`; see `data/README.md`, which also says how to switch back).

- `sitemap.csv`: the core IA (254 pages, 7 top-level items, five levels deep).
- `cobranded/`: the menus of Oceans Institute, The UWA Institute of Agriculture and UWA Public Policy Institute.
